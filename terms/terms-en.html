What Is the App

Stablue is a self-support app for moments of anxiety and emotional distress. It offers anxiety management exercises, a personal mood journal, and an AI-powered support chat. The app is operated by Liron Nir, an independent developer based in Israel.

03

Not a Substitute for Professional Care

Stablue is not a substitute for professional mental health treatment, psychological counseling, medical diagnosis, or any other professional health service.

In case of severe distress, danger to life, or suicidal thoughts — contact emergency services (101) or ERAN (1201) immediately.
