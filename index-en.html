01 About Stablue

Stablue is an emotional support app — practical tools for moments of anxiety, stress, and restlessness. The app is operated by Liron Nir, an independent developer based in Israel.

Your privacy matters to us. This document plainly explains what we collect, why, and how your information is protected.

By using the app, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use.

02 What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

  • Account details you provide, such as your email address
  • Content you choose to enter, such as mood logs and personal notes
  • App settings and usage preferences
  • Basic technical data for operation, security, and service improvement

Parts of the app are available without signing in — in that case, no data is stored outside your device.

Your notes are private: Personal notes and entries are encrypted before being stored. No one — including us — can read their contents.

03 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the service, improve your experience, maintain security, prevent misuse, and manage subscriptions and premium features.

We do not sell personal data to any third party, nor do we use it for advertising.

04 Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or transfer personal data to third parties for any commercial purpose.

05 Data Security

We invest in high-level security: data encryption, secure communications, and strict access controls. That said, no system is completely immune, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against every threat.

06 Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as it is needed to operate the service or meet legal requirements. Data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymized.
